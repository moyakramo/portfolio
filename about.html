Professional bio

Who I am

I am Moya Kramo. My background is in finance and trading, where the work is fundamentally about making decisions under uncertainty with incomplete information, and where being confidently wrong is expensive. That is the lens I bring to artificial intelligence and machine learning, which is now my primary area of study and the focus of everything on this site.

The through-line of my work is translation. Markets taught me to separate a real signal from a well-told story, and the same discipline applies to AI: distinguishing a demonstrated capability from a claimed one, and knowing which questions expose the difference. My aim is to keep building resources that let people who are not machine learning engineers evaluate these systems competently, because that judgment increasingly sits with product owners, risk functions, and executives rather than with the people writing the models.

My Approach

Signal over noise.

AI moves quickly, and separating meaningful advances from fleeting trends can be difficult. My goal is to provide clear, balanced explanations of what these technologies do, where they create measurable value, and where expectations exceed reality, so you can make informed decisions with confidence.
